## Open finding

### ATE-ADV-006 — component truth remains an ingestion assertion (Medium)

The quality rubric is now materially stronger: all ten booleans are required,
the score is recomputed, and class ranges are checked. The validator does not,
however, cross-check every component against the corresponding record evidence
for records outside numerical admission. For example, a producer can assert
`exact_model_identity: true` while leaving model identity unknown, or assert
`complete_request_breakdown: true` while declaring the breakdown incomplete.

This does not compromise the current result because all 19 fixtures are class E,
no dataset is numerically admitted, and the independent numerical-admission gate
checks the decisive identity, accounting, rights, task, and verifier evidence.
Before admitting future sources, derive mechanically checkable components from
the record and require a signed review/provenance reference for components that
cannot be inferred.

## High findings resolved during review

1. **Numerical-admission gate.** A forged A-class flag could initially pass with
   missing identity, tokens, outcome, verifier, and dataset permission. The
   validator now recomputes quality scores, requires decisive evidence fields,
   checks rights/privacy and dataset permission, and the analysis code rejects
   unpermitted datasets.
2. **Incompatible pooling.** Product surface, token scopes, measurement profile,
   cost scope, currency, price-table ID/date, and reconstruction basis were
   initially absent from compatibility keys. They are now included in both
   statistical and lookup strata.
3. **Accepted-task cost.** Initial chain logic could accept non-final acceptance,
   non-contiguous attempts, incompatible price dates, and chain-scoped totals.
   Both statistics and baselines now require one dataset/task, contiguous
   attempts, final successful acceptance, run-scoped components, and one complete
   currency/price basis.
4. **Publication boundary.** A preflight artifact initially contained private
   local paths while sitting outside the declared scan set. Its paths are now
   sanitized and the file is explicitly included by both the scanner and public
   manifest builder.
5. **Final artifact synchronization.** The corrected HTML initially postdated the
   PDF, the visual-review record was absent, and generated bytecode remained in
   the scan set. The PDF is now regenerated as 34 A4 pages, its SHA-256 matches a
   review enumerating pages 1–34, every page rendered cleanly, and no `.pyc`
   remains.

## Medium findings resolved during review

- Censored runs now use a separate rights-cleared analysis cohort rather than an
  unreachable branch inside the numerical-prior cohort.
- Success uncertainty now weights tasks equally and bootstraps task clusters;
  repeated trajectories cannot manufacture row-level precision.
- Regularized and tree diagnostics are explicitly marked
  `publication_ready: false` until grouped held-out evaluation exists.
- Uncertain prose in `failure_trajectory_coverage` is rendered as unknown rather
  than truthy/present.
- Citation validation now requires a reviewed claim mapping, and the three
  previously overbroad handbook passages were narrowed to what their cited
  primary sources support.
